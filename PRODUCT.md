# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + vanilla JS with Bootstrap 5.3 via CDN. Two static pages (landing + gallery), no build step. Light theme only (`data-bs-theme="light"`), warm cream palette with a mustard-orange accent reserved for quote CTAs (visual refresh by friend Claudio, 2026-08); later expansion to a headless CMS is planned but not part of this build.

## Users

Primary: property owners in and around Melipilla (Chile) who need pest control or gardening work — homeowners dealing with fleas, ticks, or spiders, and parcel/chacra owners or community administrators hiring landscaping, garden maintenance, or irrigation. Constructoras are NOT a stated target audience.

## Product Purpose

MeliTerra provides two service areas in Melipilla and surrounding communes: pest control (fumigations for fleas, ticks, and spiders indoors and outdoors) and gardening (landscaping, garden maintenance, and technical drip irrigation). Success for the site is a homeowner or landowner making contact — by email or phone, with a floating WhatsApp shortcut — to request a quote.

## Positioning

Local, close, and personal service: direct attention from a nearby operator, fast quoting, and proximity to Melipilla and neighboring communes. The site must communicate availability and closeness, not industrial scale.

## Operating Context

Rural-agricultural Chile (Melipilla valley). Work is quoted and arranged by email and phone. Visitors act on mobile in the field, often on their phone while standing on the land. Primary contact: email meliterrachile@gmail.com and phone +56 9 9812 2138; floating WhatsApp shortcut. Service area: Melipilla plus María Pinto, Curacaví, Alhué, El Monte, Talagante. Language: Spanish only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Two services: fumigaciones (pest control) and jardinería (paisajismo, garden maintenance, and technical irrigation). No pricing published, no e-commerce, no appointment booking. Undecided: whether MeliTerra owns machinery (user did not confirm "maquinaria propia" as a differentiator) — the site must not claim it.

## Brand Commitments

Name: MeliTerra. Green is a binding brand color and must appear throughout (user: "no olvides el verde"). Logo: `assets/logo.svg`. No tagline or slogan confirmed. 2026-08 visual refresh: warm cream palette with a mustard-orange accent (`#e8912d`) reserved exclusively for the "Cotizar"/"Solicitar cotización" CTAs, serif titles (Fraunces) over Manrope body, light theme only.

Visual direction (committed): the category-standard professional landing for local home and land-service companies, executed at high fidelity. The user explicitly rejected a themed "campo newspaper/classifieds" direction ("nada de diario del campo") and the whimsical challengers; reference bar is Chilean local service companies' sites. No ironic or thematic quirks: clean, trustworthy, human-scale.

## Evidence on Hand

A set of optimized field and service photos in `assets/img/` (JPG + WebP). No testimonials, customer names, prices, or years-in-business facts were provided and must not be fabricated. Images are referential.

## Product Principles

- Near and reachable: the quote should feel one message away (email CTA plus floating WhatsApp).
- Two clear areas with equal weight: pest control and gardening (landscaping, maintenance, irrigation).
- Honest about claims: no invented customers, machinery claims, or coverage beyond the confirmed communes.
- Mobile-first: the real visitor reads on a phone in the field.
- Clean, trustworthy, unpretentious — local service craft, not corporate construction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established. Spanish-only copy, plain semantics, contrast-safe theme pair.
