# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML + CSS + vanilla JS with Bootstrap 5.3 via CDN. Single landing page, no build step. Light/dark theme toggle via `data-bs-theme`. Decided with the user; later expansion to a headless CMS is planned but not part of this build.

## Users

Primary: property owners in and around Melipilla (Chile) who need land worked — parcel/chacra owners with private roads or yards to level, farmers and vineyard operators needing plowing and land preparation, and condominium/community administrators hiring landscaping, gardening, or irrigation work. Constructoras are NOT a stated target audience.

## Product Purpose

MeliTerra provides field and land services in Melipilla and surrounding communes: earthworks (movimientos de tierra), private road repair, plowing and agricultural land preparation, landscaping and gardening, and technical irrigation. Success for the site is a landowner or manager making contact — primarily by WhatsApp — to request a quote.

## Positioning

Local, close, and personal service: direct attention from a nearby operator, fast quoting, and proximity to Melipilla and neighboring communes. The site must communicate availability and closeness, not industrial scale.

## Operating Context

Rural-agricultural Chile (Melipilla valley). Work is quoted and arranged by WhatsApp and phone. Visitors act on mobile in the field, often on their phone while standing on the land. Primary contact: WhatsApp +56 9 9812 2138, email meliterrachile@gmail.com. Service area: Melipilla plus María Pinto, Curacaví, Alhué, El Monte, Talagante. Language: Spanish only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five services: movimientos de tierra, reparación de caminos privados, arado y preparación de tierras agrícolas, paisajismo y jardinería, riego tecnificado. No pricing published, no e-commerce, no appointment booking. Undecided: whether MeliTerra owns machinery (user did not confirm "maquinaria propia" as a differentiator) — the site must not claim it.

## Brand Commitments

Name: MeliTerra. Green is a binding brand color and must appear throughout (user: "no olvides el verde"). No logo asset exists; a simple SVG logo will be proposed. No tagline or slogan confirmed.

Visual direction (committed): the category-standard professional landing for machinery/earthmoving service companies, executed at high fidelity. The user explicitly rejected a themed "campo newspaper/classifieds" direction ("nada de diario del campo") and the whimsical challengers; reference bar is Chilean machinery companies' sites. No ironic or thematic quirks: clean, trustworthy, equipment-focused.

## Evidence on Hand

Two publicity images provided by the user in `ejemplos/` (Gemini-generated PNGs). No real photos of finished work exist in the project — gallery imagery will be generated and must be labeled as illustrative examples, not real jobs. No testimonials, customer names, prices, or years-in-business facts were provided and must not be fabricated.

## Product Principles

- Near and reachable: the quote should feel one message away (WhatsApp-first).
- Covers the whole land cycle, from soil prep to roads to gardens to irrigation.
- Honest about claims: no invented customers, machinery claims, or coverage beyond the confirmed communes.
- Mobile-first: the real visitor reads on a phone in the field.
- Clean, trustworthy, unpretentious — agricultural craft, not construction corporate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established. Spanish-only copy, plain semantics, contrast-safe theme pair.
